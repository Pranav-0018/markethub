Platform Commission Rule & Viva Quick Reference

• Every customer purchase is split cleanly: 10% Platform Commission goes to the marketplace administrator, and 90% Net Revenue is allocated to the respective merchant.
• In a multi-vendor cart with items from multiple sellers, each seller receives and fulfills only their own items.
• New sellers must receive administrator approval before listing or logging in to their dashboard.
